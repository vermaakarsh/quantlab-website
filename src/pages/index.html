    What Saarth won’t do.

    Tell you what to buy or sell

    Saarth shows evidence, ranges and trade-offs. The decision, and the reason for it, stay yours.

    Place orders on your account

    Broker connections are read-only. Saarth can place virtual paper orders for research, but cannot place orders with your broker.

    Predict the market

    Scenarios, simulations and backtests show ranges under assumptions you can see. They are research, not forecasts.

    Saarth means purposeful.

    We named it for the kind of investing we want to make easier: fewer reactions to noise, and more decisions you can still explain a year later.

    The name also echoes sārathi, the charioteer who keeps a journey on course. Chakra is a concept we are exploring for the next part of that journey.
